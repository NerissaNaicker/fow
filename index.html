How many hours should the FOW team plan for?

Plan content for eight hours for now. Although an earlier schedule offered 9.5 hours, timing at both locations was still moving; the updated WAAG should be checked as planning continues.

Yes. In the Canva overview, scroll to the right to see the year's touchpoints. The team said the design might change while the content remains the same.

Can we use the GLC Canva brand toolkit for workshop decks?

No. The toolkit is for GLC communications by chapters, regions and networks. Workshop presentation templates were still in design as of 7 October; Canva was identified as the approved workshop presentation tool.

What should the next content planning discussion focus on?

Use the evaluations to strengthen discussion, case studies, practical tools and peer learning. Review last year's deck and the content blocks in the high level agenda, then add suggestions in the “Champion Ideas” column ahead of the November champion call.
